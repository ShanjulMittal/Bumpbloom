BumpBloom — Privacy Policy & Health Data Disclosure

Effective Date: October 27, 2026  |  Application: BumpBloom — Happy Pregnancy Care

BumpBloom is designed from the ground up with local-first maternal privacy. Your pregnancy dates, supplement schedules, meal logs, hydration logs, fetal movement counts, vital signs, and doctor appointment reminders belong exclusively to you.

1. On-Device AES-256 Database Encryption (Zero Ad Tracking)

  • All personal and maternal wellness records are stored locally on your Android device inside a SQLCipher 256-bit AES encrypted database protected by keys generated inside the hardware-backed Android Keystore.
  • BumpBloom contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero data brokers, and never sells, rents, or monetizes your personal or reproductive health data.
  • Optional Biometric / PIN App Lock and Private Lock-Screen Notification Masking allow you to conceal sensitive pregnancy details on shared screens.

2. Optional Google Account & Google Drive Continuity

  • If you choose to link your Google Account or pin a Google Drive vault file (.beatusvault), BumpBloom encrypts your snapshot locally using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption before writing it to Android Auto-Backup or the specific Google Drive file you selected via the system document picker.
  • BumpBloom does not access any other files in your Google Drive and never transmits your data to developer-operated servers.

3. Android Health Connect Data Policy & Limited Use Compliance

Only when explicitly enabled by you in My Nest → Android Health Connect Sync, BumpBloom requests granular access to the following Android Health Connect data types strictly to keep your maternal wellness log and 1-page OB-GYN visit summary synchronized:

  • Hydration (Read/Write): Logs daily water and electrolyte intake against trimester hydration goals.
  • Nutrition (Read/Write): Tracks maternal energy, iron, folate, calcium, and protein intake.
  • Blood Pressure (Read/Write): Records systolic/diastolic readings for prenatal self-monitoring.
  • Blood Glucose (Read/Write): Records fasting and post-meal glucose entries for gestational wellness tracking.
  • Weight (Read/Write): Tracks maternal weight progression across trimesters.

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres strictly to the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Health Connect data is never used for advertising, creditworthiness, or shared with third parties.

4. Alarms, Notifications & User Control

  • BumpBloom uses Android exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) and notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) solely to deliver time-critical prenatal supplement reminders (including 2-hour Iron/Calcium spacing), meal/hydration nudges, and scheduled OB-GYN or ultrasound scan alerts configured by you.
  • Activating Compassionate Quiet Sanctuary Mode immediately cancels all reminders and animations in one tap.

5. Instant Data Deletion & Retention

  • Because all records reside on your device (and optionally in your own Google Drive file), you retain 100% control over retention.
  • You can permanently delete all local data at any time inside the app via My Nest → Erase All Local Phone Data, or by uninstalling BumpBloom. If you saved a .beatusvault backup in your Google Drive, you may delete that file directly from your Google Drive at any time.
Medical Wellness Disclaimer: BumpBloom is an informational maternal wellness, nutrition, and reminder companion. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, clinical treatment, or emergency obstetric advice. Always consult your obstetrician-gynecologist (OB-GYN), midwife, or qualified healthcare professional before making any medical, medication, or dietary decisions, or if you experience any concerning symptoms.

For privacy inquiries or support regarding BumpBloom, contact the developer support email listed on the official BumpBloom Google Play Store page.
