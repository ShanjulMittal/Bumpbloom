Only when explicitly enabled by you in My Nest → Android Health Connect Sync, BumpBloom requests granular access to the following Android Health Connect data types strictly to keep your maternal wellness log and 1-page OB-GYN visit summary synchronized:

  • Hydration (Read/Write): Logs daily water and electrolyte intake against trimester hydration goals.
  • Nutrition (Read/Write): Tracks maternal energy, iron, folate, calcium, and protein intake.
  • Blood Pressure (Read/Write): Records systolic/diastolic readings for prenatal self-monitoring.
  • Blood Glucose (Read/Write): Records fasting and post-meal glucose entries for gestational wellness tracking.
  • Weight (Read/Write): Tracks maternal weight progression across trimesters.

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres strictly to the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Health Connect data is never used for advertising, creditworthiness, or shared with third parties.

4. Alarms, Notifications & User Control

  • BumpBloom uses Android exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) and notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) solely to deliver time-critical prenatal supplement reminders (including 2-hour Iron/Calcium spacing), meal/hydration nudges, and scheduled OB-GYN or ultrasound scan alerts configured by you.
  • Activating Compassionate Quiet Sanctuary Mode immediately cancels all reminders and animations in one tap.

5. Instant Data Deletion & Retention

  • Because all records reside on your device (and optionally in your own Google Drive file), you retain 100% control over retention.
  • You can permanently delete all local data at any time inside the app via My Nest → Erase All Local Phone Data, or by uninstalling BumpBloom. If you saved a .beatusvault backup in your Google Drive, you may delete that file directly from your Google Drive at any time.
